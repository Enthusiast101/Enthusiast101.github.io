Anshul Kaushal

AI Researcher · M.Tech. Artificial Intelligence, IIT Patna

Hello! 👋 I study how large models work on the inside and how to keep them safe. My research covers LLM safety, mechanistic interpretability, multimodal learning and computer vision.

Across five research internships at Lexsi Labs, IIT Jodhpur, the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan, IISER Bhopal and IIIT Allahabad, I have co-authored six papers at venues including EMNLP, BMVC, The Astrophysical Journal and a NeurIPS workshop. Most recently I built SAFETUNE, a framework for auditing and repairing safety drift in fine-tuned LLMs.

If you share these interests, I'd love to talk and collaborate. 😉

Research Interests

LLM Safety & Alignment

Auditing and repairing the safety of fine-tuned language models: measuring how alignment drifts during fine-tuning, and restoring it without giving up task performance.

Mechanistic Interpretability

Reverse-engineering model internals through safety-circuit discovery, steering vectors and representation analysis, with a focus on multilingual LLM safety.

Multimodal Learning

Data-efficient image–text representation learning, such as pairing self-supervised vision encoders like DINOv2 with CLIP-style objectives for multimodal LLMs.

Computer Vision

Efficient medical image segmentation, state-space vision models (Mamba), uncertainty-aware architectures and explainable AI for visual recognition.
